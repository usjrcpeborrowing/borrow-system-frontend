Pending Faculty Confirmation: Awaiting confirmation from your class instructor.
Faculty Confirmed: Request confirmed by the instructor. Awaiting from OIC approval
OIC Approved: Request approved by the OIC. Awaiting for the assistant to release the equipment
Released: Equipment released by the assistant and given to the student.
Pending Return: Equipment is returned by student. Awaiting for assistant's confirmation
Returned: Returned equipment checked and confirmed by the students. Assistant may update the equipments condition and quantity upon checking of the returned equipments
Unreturned: When equipment is still unreturned past the class period. System will automatically update it.
Rejected: Request denied by the OIC.
System Reset: Disregarded items in Pending Approval and Approved (Unreleased) that were not yet released reset by the system itself.
