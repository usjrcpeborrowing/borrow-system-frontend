Pending Approval: Awaiting approval.
Approved (Unreleased): Approved but not yet released.
Rejected: Request denied.
Released: Item given to the student.
Pending Return: Awaiting confirmation of return.
Returned: Item successfully returned.
Unreturned: Not returned on time.
System Reset: Disregarded items in Pending Approval and Approved (Unreleased) that were not yet released reset by the system itself.
